In this essay I will explain the main causes of the First World War. Economic rivalry between Britain and Germany over trade and industrial output added to the distrust between the two nations. The decline of the Ottoman Empire left a power vacuum in the Balkans that Austria-Hungary and Russia both wanted to influence. Mobilisation plans were so rigid that once Russia began to mobilise, Germany felt it had to act quickly. Diplomatic efforts such as the Hague conferences failed to produce meaningful limits on armaments. Leaders miscalculated by assuming that any war would be over quickly and would not be costly. In conclusion, the First World War had many causes and they were all connected.
