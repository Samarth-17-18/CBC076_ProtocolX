In this essay I will explain the main causes of the First World War. The system of alliances divided Europe into two armed camps, the Triple Entente and the Triple Alliance, so a small conflict could quickly involve many countries. Many young men in every country were enthusiastic about fighting and expected a short war. Nationalism was especially strong in the Balkans, where Slavic groups wanted freedom from Austria-Hungary. The Schlieffen Plan required Germany to attack France quickly through Belgium, which brought Britain into the war. The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand in Sarajevo on 28 June 1914 was the immediate trigger for the war. Newspapers in many countries also encouraged national pride and hostility towards rivals. In conclusion, the First World War had many causes and they were all connected.
